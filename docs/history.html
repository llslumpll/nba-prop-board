❓ WHAT DO THESE WORDS MEAN? tap to open
Hit rate
How often the pick was right. Voids (player didn't play) and pushes (landed exactly on the line) don't count either way.
Needed to profit (break-even)
The hit rate the price requires. At −110 you need 52.4%; PrizePicks 6-pick flex about 54.2%. Hitting 55% at a price that needs 57% still loses money.
Units (U)
Bet size. 1U is your normal bet. Flat 1U = what you'd have made betting 1 unit on everything. At our units = betting the recommended size shown next to each pick.
Recommended units
0.25U low (0+ pts over break-even)0.5U medium (2+ pts over break-even)0.75U lean (4+ pts over break-even)1U confident (6+ pts over break-even)1.5U high (9+ pts over break-even)2U extreme (13+ pts over break-even) PASS chance doesn't beat the price
Value / edge
Our chance minus the break-even, in percentage points. 60% on a pick that needs 52.4% = 7.6 points of value.
Closing line value (CLV)
Did the line move our way after we froze the pick? Beating the closing line most of the time is the earliest sign of a real edge.
Best 5 / Best Value
Best 5 = highest chance on each list. Best Value = biggest edge over the price. Both lock at tip.
Lean / edge
Every stat is a "lean" until its graded record beats the line; then it's labelled "edge".
