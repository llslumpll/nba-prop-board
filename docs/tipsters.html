    How it works

    • Who is tracked: only accounts whose full public record beat the market since opening night and who post their losses (Dr. Profit, WizBetz, and since Nov 19 byrnetts, PeakyProps, tipsterdiogo and IzzoBets, found in a search of free accounts; IzzoBets never posts his misses, but every pick he posted before tip is graded here), plus Goblin's spread legs and the safest (lowest) tier of Sam's daily lotto ladder, tagged SHORT PRICE when it pays +210 or less. Checked on Nov 18; anyone can be added or dropped later.
    • Logging: their picks are added when you ask Claude to log them, with the time each was posted and the odds they posted. Picks posted after tip are not counted.
    • Tail test: the first build that sees a pick before its game tips records the best price you could get right then (Kalshi or Polymarket US at the same number, or FanDuel at the same line), and the pick is also graded at that price. It answers "does tailing pay for me".
    • Cushion combos: their props softened by a per-stat cushion to the Kalshi rung costing 75–92%, plus 75%+ favorites to win, stacked safest first into about +400, +550 and +700 combos, locked at the first tip. Before Nov 20 it was one rung safer at 65–82%, 2–4 legs (kept as "old one-rung combo").
    • Grading: final box scores, the same night. A push or a player who doesn't play is voided. Nothing is ever removed.
    • Safe lines: @PrizePicksTacos' daily list of very safe lines, logged with his posted odds and graded the same night. Tonight's lines are priced at the same number on Kalshi / Polymarket US (or FanDuel at the same line); the first price seen before tip is kept so the list is also graded at your price.
    • Stakes: none. Everything here is tracking only until a tipster has 50+ tail-test picks with a profit at your price.
