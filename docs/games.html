How game picks work
Our team score is the sum of our player projections. Each game pick starts from what the market implies (FanDuel's spread for the margin, its total for the points) and moves toward our number by up to 25%, scaled by how well our team numbers have tracked the market so far (correlation, pooled over every game). Early in a season, when rosters are new, that agreement is low, so our weight is small and most picks sit near the market; Best 5 and Best Value only list picks whose chance beats their price. Totals use how each game differs from the night's average, since our summed scores run high early. Spread and total chances are capped at 75%. On the 2025-26 season our summed scores picked 64.0% of winners with a 16.32-point spread of misses. All game picks are leans until History shows an edge.
