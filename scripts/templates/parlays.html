    • Where the legs come from: picks the site has already recorded for tonight (the same ones on Picks and Games), never a SKIP, and only games that haven't started.
    • One leg per game. Legs from the same game move together (a blowout hurts everyone's minutes), which makes a parlay riskier than its chance says.

    • Locking: until the first leg's game tips, a parlay can swap legs as lines and injuries move. From that tip on, its legs, lines and sides are frozen and graded exactly as shown.
    • Grading: a leg whose player doesn't play, or that lands exactly on the line, drops out and the parlay pays as the smaller one (most books do this). A parlay is LOST the moment one leg misses.
    • Price check: GOOD PRICE when chance all hit × payout is above 1 (worth it on average), PRICE TOO HIGH when the payout is too small for how likely it is. ALL LEGS ON KALSHI = every leg can be placed on Kalshi; the leg shows the exact Kalshi market (OVER 2.5 = YES on 3+, UNDER 2.5 = NO on 3+).
    • Chance all hit = the legs' chances multiplied. Pays about = each leg's own price multiplied, how sportsbooks and Kalshi-style combos roughly price them; PrizePicks pays from a fixed table that can be lower.
