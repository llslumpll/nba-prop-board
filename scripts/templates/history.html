Profit & closing line value

Units: 1 unit on every graded pick at the price of its own line (FanDuel juice, Kalshi ask, PrizePicks 54.2% flex break-even, so PrizePicks units are an approximation). Closing line value (CLV): did the line move our way between when we froze the pick and tip-off? Beating the close on most picks is the earliest sign of a real edge: it shows up after about 100 picks, long before a hit rate means much.

Monthly retune

On the 1st of each month the model's settings are re-fit with this season's games included, then tested on the most recent 30% of game days, which the new settings never saw. New settings are adopted only if their average miss there is at least 0.5% lower and no main stat gets more than 1% worse.
