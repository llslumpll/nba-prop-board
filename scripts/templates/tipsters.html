How it works

  • Who is tracked: only accounts whose full public record beat the market since opening night and who post their losses (Dr. Profit, WizBetz), plus Goblin's spread legs and the safest (lowest) tier of Sam's daily lotto ladder, tagged SHORT PRICE when it pays +210 or less. Checked on Nov 18; anyone can be added or dropped later.
  • Logging: their picks are added when you ask Claude to log them, with the time each was posted and the odds they posted. Picks posted after tip are not counted.
  • Tail test: the first build that sees a pick before its game tips records the best price you could get right then (Kalshi at the same number, or FanDuel at the same line), and the pick is also graded at that price. It answers "does tailing pay for me".
  • Cushion combo: 2–4 of their prop overs moved one Kalshi rung safer, legs costing 65–82%, locked at the first tip. It tests your alt-line idea on picks from people with a real record.
  • Grading: final box scores, the same night. A push or a player who doesn't play is voided. Nothing is ever removed.
  • Stakes: none. Everything here is tracking only until a tipster has {{ tp.earn_n }}+ tail-test picks with a profit at your price.
